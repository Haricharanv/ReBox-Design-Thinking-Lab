💡 3 Practical Ways to Demonstrate This Physically in Class

▼
🏷️ 1. The "Box Passport" Tracker

Stick a "ReBox Tracker Passport" label onto a physical box. Draw 7 checkbox slots (Loop 1 to Loop 7) directly on it. During the presentation, show the box and explain that every time a resident drops it in the Green Bin, the society guard or bot stamps/initials the next checkbox, letting the next shopkeeper know its exact wear level.

🩹 2. Tape Tear-off Demonstration

Bring two box flaps to class. On one, seal it with standard brown plastic packing tape and rip it open—showing how it tears the box's outer kraft fibers and ruins the box. On the other, demonstrate Water-Activated Gummed Paper Tape or a reusable elastic band/velcro strap, showing how flaps can be sealed and unsealed 7+ times without ripping the cardboard structure.

🏗️ 3. The Flattening & Reassembly Speedrun

Demonstrate how e-commerce boxes are made with slotted flaps. Show how flattening the box without cutting the flaps preserves the integrity of the corners. Challenge a student to flatten a box under 5 seconds and re-erect it, showing how it can be stored flat in society basements and rebuilt for shopkeepers instantly.
