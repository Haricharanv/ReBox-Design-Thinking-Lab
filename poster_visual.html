THE PROBLEM

Urban residents lack a convenient system to dispose of intact e-commerce boxes. Concurrently, local vendors face high recurring costs purchasing new boxes for their daily operations.

DEFINE

Problem Statement

Urban residents and vendors need a hyper-local, seamless system to redirect usable e-commerce corrugated boxes from landfills directly to nearby businesses, thereby creating immediate economic and environmental value.

POV

We believe that an integrated hyper-local network can redirect perfectly usable corrugated boxes from landfills directly to nearby businesses.

Key Beneficiaries

  • Urban Apartment Residents
  • Local Kirana Stores
  • E-commerce Platforms
  • Municipal Waste Mgmt.
