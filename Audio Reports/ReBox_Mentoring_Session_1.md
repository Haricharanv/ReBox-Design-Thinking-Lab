### 2. Constructive Feedback & Guidance

Prof. Chakravorty provided the following constructive feedback and guidance to enhance the team's project execution:

*   **Team Collaboration and Presence:** It was strongly advised that all team members must be present for discussions with the mentor, especially for core project work like DTL (Design Thinking Lab) activities. Prof. Chakravorty reiterated that effective progress and comprehensive understanding require the full team's participation in these critical interactions. This approach ensures that all members are fully informed and contribute to shared decisions.
*   **Accountability and Shared Responsibility:** The team was encouraged to foster an environment of complete transparency and shared responsibility. Prof. Chakravorty advised against creating situations where individual members might feel uninformed or excluded from discussions, emphasizing the importance of proactive collaboration to prevent any misunderstandings or perceived blame.
*   **Direct Communication:** The team was guided to communicate directly and clearly, especially regarding attendance and project updates.
*   **Fieldwork and Data Collection Strategy:**
    *   **Joint Efforts for Distant Locations:** For fieldwork requiring visits to distant locations, the team was strongly encouraged to travel together. This is to ensure safety, mutual support, and a unified approach to data collection.
    *   **Work Division:** Prof. Chakravorty stressed the importance of dividing tasks clearly and effectively among team members to optimize efficiency and ensure comprehensive coverage of all aspects of the project. This includes assigning specific categories for survey work to each member.
*   **Broadening Customer Perspective:** The team received guidance to look beyond immediate circles and consider a wider range of potential customers or stakeholders. Prof. Chakravorty encouraged observing their surroundings and recognizing that many individuals within the environment (e.g., people at canteens, those giving/receiving orders) could be valuable sources of insight for the ReBox project. This broadens the scope for relevant data collection.
*   **External Discussion for Insights:** To gather unbiased perspectives and richer data, the team was advised to discuss project ideas and survey findings with people outside their immediate classmates. This includes engaging with faculty members and various individuals in the surrounding environment who could offer diverse viewpoints, helping the team avoid internal biases.
*   **Meeting Logistics:** All team meetings with the mentor should take place in the designated lab.
