A Circular Economy System for E-Commerce Packaging

Redirecting intact boxes from apartment residents to local vendors

The Problem

Every day, 15 million e-commerce parcels are shipped in India

Most structurally intact boxes are crushed by scrap dealers for pulp recycling, while local vendors spend Rs.20-35 buying new boxes. There is zero organized reuse infrastructure at the apartment level.

Phase 2: Define

Defined Problem & HMW Statement

5 Whys Root Cause

  • Why landfill? Thrown in dry waste
  • Why throw? No space, no alternative
  • Why no alternative? Irregular kabadiwala
  • Why no redirect? Weight-based selling to pulp
  • ROOT: System optimized for RECYCLING not REUSE

POV Statements

Shopper: Urban residents who shop online NEED a zero-friction disposal method BECAUSE space constraints force rapid disposal while eco-guilt makes them wish for better.

Vendor: Local vendors NEED consistent, hyper-local affordable boxes BECAUSE new packaging at Rs.20-35/box consumes 10-15% of margins.

HMW Statement

"How Might We intercept structurally intact e-commerce boxes at the apartment level and redirect them to local vendors who need them — making disposal effortless for residents and supply reliable for shopkeepers?"

Phase 3: Ideate

Worst Possible Idea + Challenge Assumptions

The Worst Idea

  • Require 200MB app download
  • Force weighing each box on postal scale
  • Schedule a 2-hour pickup window
  • Charge residents Rs.50/box
  • Print a 3-page form for each drop-off

Flipped Design Principles

  • Instead of a 200MB app: Zero download (WhatsApp only)
  • Instead of weighing each box: Just drop, no measurement
  • Instead of a 2-hour window: 24/7 bin access
  • Instead of charging Rs.50: Free for residents
  • Instead of a 3-page form: One QR scan, 3 seconds
Phase 3: Ideate

Storm Techniques

Body Storm

Physically enacted box drop-off journey in a real basement.

Insight: Bin MUST be visible from lift lobby. Out of sight = out of mind.

Game Storm

Designed points-based game: earn "ReBox Points," unlock badges, compete on leaderboard.

Insight: Gamification works for 20-30 age, not for 40+ who prefer intrinsic motivation.

Cheat Storm

"What if Amazon pre-paid Rs.5 reverse deposit?" Led to exploring EPR credit integration. "What if boxes teleported?" Led to WhatsApp instant-alert system.

Crowd Storm

Posted problem on class WhatsApp group (45 students). Received 23 unique suggestions in 48 hours. Top ideas adopted: partner with maintenance staff, add a flattener tool, monthly "thank you" card.

Phase 3: Ideate

Final Solution: Layered Circular System

Layer 1: Physical Green Bin

Corrugated-only bin in apartment basements. Zero tech required. Designed for immediate convenience and older demographic adoption.

Layer 2: WhatsApp Bot Interface

QR scan redirects to WhatsApp. Autonomously registers drop-offs, calculates personal carbon savings ledger, and broadcasts size-sorted pickup alerts to local vendors.

Layer 3: Dashboard App (Scalable Future)

Unified database dashboards for Shoppers (impact scorecards, leaderboards), Vendors (pickup routing, stock levels), and RWA Admins.
