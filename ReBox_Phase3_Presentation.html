1. Empathy to Ideation (HMW Statement)

Our Guiding HMW Question:

"How Might We intercept structurally intact e-commerce boxes at the apartment level and redirect them to local vendors who need them — making disposal effortless for residents and supply reliable for shopkeepers?"

Design Directives:

  • Zero-Friction for Shoppers: Must fit into their existing routines (discarding on the way out).
  • Financial Value for Vendors: Lower their packaging overhead from ₹25 to <₹5 per box.
  • No Large Downloads: Avoid asking users to download heavy apps initially.

4. The Selected Layered Solution

Layer 1: The Green Bin

Basement drop-off bins + posters. Zero technology barriers. Ensures 100% accessibility for elderly or non-tech residents.

Layer 2: WhatsApp Bot

QR code on bin triggers WhatsApp. Users logs box drops and earns points. Guards log vendor pickups via automated chat prompts.

Layer 3: Digital App

Future mobile portal. Tracks community diversion stats, vendor demand broadcasts, and carbon emission scorecard dashboards.

5. Storyboard & User Flow

The cycle of corrugated cardboard reuse in the local community:

StageUser PathSystem Event
1. SupplyResident flattens box, takes to basement bin.None.
2. LogResident scans Bin QR, logs drop on WhatsApp.WhatsApp Bot updates inventory, assigns points.
3. BroadcastVendor searches for boxes or requests supply.System alerts nearby vendors of stock.
4. CollectVendor visits society, picks up boxes.Security guard verifies collection via WhatsApp button.
5. Close LoopVendor reuses boxes for fruit/groceries.Database logs a successful circle of reuse.

6. High-Fidelity Interactive Prototype

Built directly as an interactive web demo mapping the three circular views:

Resident & Shopper Portal

  • Scan QR to simulate society green bin drop-off.
  • Shows real-time carbon offset tracker and points leaderboard.
  • Simulated WhatsApp Bot prompts.

Shopkeeper & Guard Portal

  • Interactive list of nearby vendor packaging demands.
  • Verification button for guards to dispatch inventory.
  • Toggle views easily to demo to stakeholders.

7. Demonstrating the 5-7 Reuse Lifecycle

How we prove box durability and tracking in our presentation:

1. Interactive Digital Simulator

  • Live demonstration of a single box's 7-cycle custody chain.
  • Visualizes progressive wear-and-tear (tape layering, stamps, scuffs).
  • Calculates cumulative savings: 7.7 kg CO₂, 1,400 L Water, 116 kWh Energy.

2. Physical Classroom Props

  • "Box Passport" Tracker: Physical box with printed checkboxes R1 to R7 for tracking cycle counts.
  • Gummed Paper Tape Demo: Show how water-activated tape seals flaps securely without tearing kraft fibers upon opening.

8. Testing & Pilot Validation Plan

Launching a 2-week pilot at Bellandur Green Society (200 flats) and 5 local vendors:

Key Evaluation Metrics:

  • Diverted Cardboard %: Bins vs municipal dry waste.
  • Vendor Savings: Reduction in packaging costs.
  • User Dropoff Time: Duration to complete WhatsApp drop logs.

Feedback Mechanism:

  • Resident post-use WhatsApp feedback.
  • Bi-weekly vendor check-ins regarding box structural qualities.
  • Security guard operational review sessions.
