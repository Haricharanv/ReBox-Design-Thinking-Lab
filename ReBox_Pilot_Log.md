## 📈 Key Findings from Pilot
1. **Quality Check Success:** 100% of the 42 boxes dropped off by residents were in "Like-New" or "Good" condition and immediately accepted by vendors.
2. **Vendor Demand:** Vendor demand actually exceeded supply. Sri Krishna Groceries requested 10 extra boxes on Day 4 that could not be fulfilled.
3. **Resident Habit:** 4 of the 18 residents used the drop-off bin multiple times within the 5 days, proving high retention for the WhatsApp bot method.
