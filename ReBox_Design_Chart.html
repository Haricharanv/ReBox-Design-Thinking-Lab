📦 ReBox

A Sustainable Circular Economy Initiative for E-commerce Packaging

🛑 The Problem: Linear Economy

E-commerce relies heavily on single-use corrugated cardboard boxes. Once delivered, these boxes are immediately discarded, contributing massively to municipal solid waste and deforestation.

  • Millions of trees cut annually for packaging.
  • High carbon footprint in manufacturing.
  • End up in landfills after 1 use.

✅ The Solution: Circular Economy

ReBox transforms packaging into a reusable asset. By utilizing smart-tracking (QR codes) and leveraging local shopkeepers as collection hubs, we create a closed-loop system.

  • Boxes reused 10-20 times.
  • Gamified rewards for customers.
  • New revenue stream for local shopkeepers.

👥 Value for Customers

Customers are incentivized to participate in the circular economy rather than throwing boxes away.

  • Eco-conscious: Feel good about reducing carbon footprint.
  • Incentives: Earn "ReBox Coins" via the app.
  • Convenience: Drop-off points are nearby.

🏪 Value for Shopkeepers

Local shops act as the critical nodes (micro-hubs) of the network.

  • Extra Income: Paid a small fee for every box collected and stored.
  • Footfall: Customers drop a box, buy groceries.
  • Space efficiency: Smart folding design.
