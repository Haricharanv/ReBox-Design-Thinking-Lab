Questionnaire 1 — Urban Resident / Online Shopper PRIMARY PERSONA

Target: Apartment residents who receive 4+ e-commerce deliveries per month · Duration: ~10 minutes

Purpose: Understand the pain points, motivations, and behavioral barriers of residents who receive e-commerce packages. Validate demand for a frictionless, in-building box drop-off solution and gauge willingness to participate in ReBox.
Section A

Respondent Background

1
What is your name, age group, and occupation?
Helps us understand the demographic profile of our user base.
Open-ended
2
How many e-commerce deliveries (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, etc.) do you receive in a typical month?
3
How many people live in your household?
Section B

Current Cardboard Disposal Behavior

4
Walk me through exactly what happens after you open an e-commerce package. What do you do with the cardboard box?
Look for keywords: pile up, balcony, kabadiwala, trash, guilt.
Open-ended · Deep Probe
5
How long does a cardboard box typically sit in your home before you dispose of it?
6
Where does the cardboard ultimately end up most of the time?
7
How do you feel when you throw away a perfectly intact Amazon box? Describe the emotion.
Probe for guilt, indifference, frustration, helplessness. Don't lead.
Emotional probe
8
Have you ever tried to give away or recycle cardboard boxes in an organised way? What stopped you?
Barrier probe
Section C

ReBox Concept Validation

9

Questionnaire 2 — Local Vendor / Small Business DEMAND SIDE

Target: Fruit sellers, grocery shops, bakeries, movers, packers within 1 km of residential areas · Duration: ~8 minutes

Purpose: Quantify the financial pain of cardboard procurement for small vendors, understand box quality requirements, and validate willingness to adopt a neighbourhood supply of reused boxes.
Section A

Business Background & Packaging Needs

1
What type of business do you run, and how many years have you been operating?
Open-ended
2
How many cardboard boxes do you use per week on average?
3
Where do you currently source your cardboard boxes from?
4
How much do you spend per week on cardboard packaging? (In ?)
5
Have cardboard prices increased in the last 2 years? How has it affected your business?
Open-ended · Pain probe
Section B

Attitude Toward Reused Boxes

6
Would you use second-hand Amazon / Flipkart boxes if they were structurally intact and clean?
7
What is the maximum price per box you would pay for good-quality reused boxes? (vs. ?20–35 new)
8
What would make you reject a donated box? (Select all that apply)
9
What sizes of boxes do you need most? (Describe or select)
Section C

ReBox Model Validation

Questionnaire 3 — RWA / Society Administrator ENABLER

Target: Resident Welfare Association (RWA) presidents, secretaries, and facilities managers · Duration: ~10 minutes

Purpose: Understand the operational feasibility of installing ReBox bins in a society, identify gatekeeping barriers, and assess willingness to officially adopt ReBox as a society-level sustainability initiative.

Society Profile & Waste Management

How big a problem is cardboard / packaging waste within your society? (1 = Not a problem, 5 = Major problem)
