ReBox Circular System

A hyper-local circular economy model designed to intercept corrugated boxes at the apartment level and redirect them to nearby local vendors.

🧩 Market Segmentation

Dividing the ecosystem into actionable customer groups. Use the toggle below to switch between the supply side (residents) and demand side (vendors).

📍

Geographic Segmentation

• High-density urban residential complexes (gated societies with 100+ apartments).
• Located in primary commercial clusters of Tier-1/2 Indian cities (e.g. Bangalore, Hyderabad).
• Target micro-zones: areas with active delivery nodes like Bellandur, HSR Layout, Koramangala.

👥

Demographic Segmentation

• Age: 21–45 years old.
• Occupations: IT professionals, students, active families, corporate workers.
• Income: Middle to upper-middle class with high digital purchasing power.
• Users frequently order online (packages arriving multiple times per week).

🧠

Psychographic Segmentation

• Environmentally conscious but time-poor; feels "eco-guilt" about packing waste.
• Clutter-averse: values a tidy house/balcony and wishes to declutter quickly.
• Responds to community recognition, green leaderboards, and immediate feedback loops.

⚡

Behavioral Segmentation

• Shopping pattern: High frequency of online ordering (e-commerce + quick commerce).
• Disposal pattern: Defaults to municipal dry waste bin because traditional recycling is too inconvenient.
• Friction tolerance: Extremely low; won't sort unless bin is immediately accessible (basement lobby).

🎯 Targeting Strategy

Selecting and prioritizing segments where the circular system can deliver maximum early adoption and volume density.

Primary Target: High-Density Gated Communities

Primary Market

Gated societies of 200+ units represent the highest density of cardboard box generation. Intercepting waste here offers logistical efficiency and immediate community momentum.

  • Volume Concentration: A single building generates 100+ structurally intact boxes weekly, making pickup routing viable for nearby shopkeepers.
  • Structured Administration: Society management provides physical spaces (lobbies/basements) and guard supervision.
  • Homogeneous Audience: Tech-literate residents who value eco-gamification (society metrics) and appreciate clutter removal.

Demand Target: Local Retailers & kiranas

Demand Market

Small retail businesses located within a 500m to 1km radius of apartment nodes, facing high operational costs on secondary packaging materials.

  • Kirana & Fruit Shops: Highest packaging usage. Spending ₹500–₹5,000+ monthly on brand-neutral boxes.
  • Logistics Courier Outlets: Value clean boxes for secondary customer packaging, extremely receptive to local low-cost boxes.
  • Hyper-Local Radius: Proximity ensures vendors are willing to perform self-pickup runs or accept direct drop-offs.
