User Journey Mapping

Visualize the online shopper's complete experience from package arrival to ReBox participation

⏱ 25–30 minutes

🎒 Materials Needed

  • 📄 1 large chart paper (A1 or A2)
  • 📌 Sticky notes (yellow, pink, blue, green — 1 pad each)
  • ✏️ 3 thick markers (black, red, green)
  • 📏 Ruler or straight edge
  • 🖊️ Pens for writing on sticky notes

📝 Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Draw the Grid

Use a black marker + ruler to draw a grid on the chart paper. 6 columns for the journey stages and 4 rows for the dimensions. Leave a header row at the top and a label column on the left.

2

Write Column Headers

Label the 6 columns in bold marker: 📦 Package Arrives → 😟 Clutter Builds → 😔 Eco-Guilt → 🟢 Discovers Bin → 📱 Scans QR → 🌱 Feels Good

3

Write Row Labels

Label the 4 rows on the left: ACTIONS (blue stickies) · THOUGHTS (green stickies) · EMOTIONS (yellow stickies) · PAIN POINTS (pink stickies)

4

Write & Place Sticky Notes

Each team member writes content on sticky notes (one idea per note) and places them in the correct cell. Use the pre-filled template below for the exact text to write.

5

Draw the Emotion Curve

Use a RED marker to draw a curved line across the top of the chart showing emotion highs and lows: Start HIGH (excited) → dip LOW (frustrated/guilty) → rise back to HIGH (satisfied/proud).

Brain Dump + Affinity Mapping

Silent brainstorming followed by clustering ideas into themes

⏱ 15–20 minutes (5 min dump + 10 min clustering)

🎒 Materials Needed

  • 📌 Sticky notes (3 pads, multiple colors)
  • ✏️ 3 thick Sharpie/markers (1 per person)
  • 📄 1 large chart paper or blank wall space
  • 🖊️ 1 dark marker for cluster headings

📝 Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Set the Prompt

Write this question at the top of the chart paper in bold: "What are all the problems, ideas, and insights related to e-commerce packaging reuse?"

2

Silent Brain Dump (5 minutes)

Set a timer. Everyone writes as many ideas as possible — ONE idea per sticky note, large clear handwriting. No talking, no filtering. Quantity > Quality.

3

Stick & Read Aloud

Everyone sticks their notes on the chart paper randomly. Take turns reading each note aloud briefly (5 seconds per note max).

4

Cluster into Themes (Affinity Mapping)

As a team, physically move sticky notes that are similar close to each other. You should end up with 4–5 clusters. Draw a circle around each cluster and give it a heading.

Multi-Voting

Democratic dot-voting to converge on the winning solution

⏱ 10–15 minutes

🎒 Materials Needed

  • 📄 1 chart paper (draw the voting grid)
  • 🔴🔵🟢 Round dot stickers (5 per person) — or use markers
  • ✏️ Markers for the grid
  • 📏 Ruler for clean lines

📝 Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Draw the Voting Grid

Create a table on chart paper with these columns: Solution Idea | Hari | Anjali | Supriya | Total | Decision

2

List the 4 Solutions

Write each solution in a row: (1) Physical Bins Only, (2) WhatsApp Bot + QR Bins, (3) Dedicated Mobile App, (4) SMS-only Broadcast

3

Each Person Votes (5 dots each)

Each member gets 5 dot stickers (or draws 5 dots with markers). Place dots in your column next to the solutions you support. You CAN put multiple dots on one solution!

4

Count & Declare Winner

Add up dots per row. The highest score wins. Circle it with a GREEN marker and write "✅ SELECTED" next to it.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Everyone votes simultaneously to avoid bias
  • Use different colored dot stickers per person so you can trace votes
  • If you don't have dot stickers, draw stars (★) or tally marks with markers
  • Highlight the winner with a big green border — it should pop visually

Crazy 8s

Rapid-fire sketching of 8 solution ideas in 8 minutes

⏱ Exactly 8 minutes (1 min per box)

🎒 Materials Needed

  • 📄 1 blank A4 sheet per person
  • ✏️ 1 black marker or pen per person
  • ⏱ Timer (phone timer)

📝 Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Fold the Paper

Take a blank A4 sheet. Fold it in half (landscape). Fold in half again. And again. When unfolded, you have 8 rectangles.

2

Set the Timer — 8 Minutes

Announce: "You have 8 minutes. Sketch one distinct ReBox solution idea per box. 1 minute per box. Stick figures and rough sketches are PERFECT. Go!"

3

Sketch Rapidly

Each box should have: a quick sketch/doodle + a 2–3 word title. No perfection needed. The goal is speed and variety.

4

Share & Discuss

When the timer rings, each person holds up their sheet and walks through their 8 sketches in under 2 minutes.

Storyboard / Analogies

A 6-panel comic strip narrating the complete ReBox user journey

⏱ 20–25 minutes

🎒 Materials Needed

  • 📄 1 large chart paper (A2 preferred)
  • ✏️ Black marker for outlines
  • 🖍️ Colored markers/pencils for fills
  • 📏 Ruler for panel borders
  • 📝 Pencil for drafting first

📝 Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Draw 6 Panels

Divide the chart paper into 6 large rectangles (2 rows × 3 columns). Each panel should be roughly equal in size. Draw thick borders with a ruler.

2

Pencil Draft First

Lightly sketch each scene in pencil before committing with markers. This lets you fix proportions and add speech bubbles.

3

Ink & Color

Go over the pencil with black marker for outlines. Add color to key elements: the green bin, the phone screen, the happy vendor. Write captions below each panel.

4

Add the Analogy Banner

At the bottom of the chart, write in bold: "ReBox is like a Library for Boxes — borrow, use, return, repeat."

📐 Storyboard Template (Draw these 6 scenes)

📦🚪
Panel 1 — Supply
Delivery Arrives

Priya (resident) receives her Amazon/Flipkart package at her apartment door. She unboxes the item — the cardboard box is perfectly intact.

😟📦📦📦
Panel 2 — Problem
Clutter & Guilt

Boxes pile up on Priya's balcony for days. She feels guilty throwing away a perfectly good box. "Someone must need this..."

🟢📦⬇️
Panel 3 — Discovery
The Green Bin

Priya spots the ReBox green bin in the basement (next to the lift). She flattens the box and drops it in — zero effort, on her way to parking.

📱💬✅
Panel 4 — Log
QR Scan → WhatsApp

Priya scans the QR code on the bin. WhatsApp opens automatically. The bot says: "Box logged! You've saved 0.48 kg CO₂ 🌱"

🏪👨‍🌾📦
Panel 5 — Collect
Vendor Pickup

Ramesh (fruit vendor, 500m away) gets a WhatsApp alert: "15 boxes ready at Green Valley Apts." He walks over and picks them up for ₹2–5 each.

🍎📦😊🔄
Panel 6 — Reuse
Circle Complete

Ramesh uses the box to pack fruits for his customers. The box starts its 2nd life. Priya's clutter → Ramesh's savings. Loop closed! ♻️

🔗 Analogy to Write on the Chart Paper

"ReBox is like a Library for Boxes 📚📦
Just like a library — you borrow a book, read it, return it, and someone else uses it.
With ReBox — you receive a box, unbox it, drop it in the bin, and a vendor reuses it."
