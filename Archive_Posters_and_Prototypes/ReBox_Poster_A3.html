Problem Statement

Structurally intact e-commerce boxes are destroyed daily while nearby vendors pay premium for new ones — because zero infrastructure connects box-generators with box-needers at the neighbourhood level.

Root Cause (5 Whys)

  • Boxes end up in landfills → Residents throw them in dry waste (65%)
  • No easy alternative → Kabadiwala visits are irregular
  • Kabadiwala sells by weight → Crushing is more profitable than reuse
  • ROOT: Scrap ecosystem is optimized for recycling (destroy), not reuse (redirect intact)

Pain Point Summary

  • Shoppers: Space constraints → eco-guilt → forced disposal
  • Vendors: ₹20-35/box cost → travel to wholesale → capital lock-up
  • Gap: ₹20 price gap between scrap (₹3) and new box (₹25) with no bridge

The Layered Circular Solution

Layer 1 — Physical Green Bin 📦

Designated cardboard-only bin in apartment basements. Zero-friction entry point. Poster guides residents to flatten & drop. Serves tech-averse users like Swarna, 45.

Layer 2 — WhatsApp Bot + QR 💬

QR code on bin opens WhatsApp chat. Resident logs drop-off, gets impact score. Bot broadcasts "10 medium boxes ready" to nearby registered vendors. Guard verifies pickup.

Layer 3 — Dashboard (Future) 📊

Vendor portal to broadcast box needs. Admin dashboard for diversion analytics. Carbon offset tracking for residents. Planned for Phase 2 scaling.
