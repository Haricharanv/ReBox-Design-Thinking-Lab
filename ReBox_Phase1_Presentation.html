Problem Statement

Urban households consistently throw away perfectly good e-commerce boxes into the trash, while local fruit and vegetable vendors nearby spend a lot of money buying new cardboard boxes. This shows a broken system where we waste boxes that could easily be reused locally.

Assumptions

Our Assumptions (To Validate)

  • 1
    Urban households feel a bit guilty about throwing away perfectly strong boxes, but lack an easy alternative to dispose of them usefully.
  • 2
    Local vendors (fruit sellers, packers & movers, bakers) are willing to use clean, second-hand branded boxes (e.g., Amazon, Myntra) if it significantly saves them money.
  • 3
    Apartment security guards or housekeeping staff can act as aggregation nodes for a small incentive or through a structured bin system.
  • 4
    The total number of boxes coming into a standard 100-flat apartment society is enough to meet the demand of nearby small businesses.
  • 5
    Traditional Kabadiwalas predominantly care about the weight of cardboard for paper pulping, not preserving the structural utility of the box.

Initial Persona (Draft)

Meet Priya, The Frequent Shopper

?????

Priya Sharma

26 years old � Software Engineer � Lives in a 200-flat Society (Bellandur)

Situation

Orders on Amazon, Myntra, and Instamart 3-5 times a week. Has a dedicated corner in her balcony piled high with flattened cardboard boxes and empty bubble wrap. Doesn't have the time to negotiate with a kabadiwala.

?? Pain Points

Feels intense guilt throwing away structurally perfect boxes into the main garbage. But keeping them looks messy and takes up premium apartment space. Wants them gone, but wishes they were actually reused.

?? Goals

Wants an "easy" way to drop off her boxes within her society compound and know they are actually being reused by someone who needs them, not just shredded.

Current Solutions & Gaps

Existing Workarounds & Why They Fail

?? Informal Kabadiwalas

Local Scrap Dealers

They buy per KG to send to pulping mills. They don't care about the box's shape�they crush it.

GAP: Destroys the structural utility of the box

??? Municipal Dry Waste

BBMP City Transport

Gets mixed with plastics and dirt, ending up in landfills or high-energy recycling plants.

GAP: Energy-heavy recycling, not direct reuse

?? Wholesale Box Manufacturers

Suppliers for Vendors

Provide brand new boxes, but require bulk purchases and are highly vulnerable to paper price inflations.

GAP: Too expensive for micro-vendors

?? ReBox (Our Concept)

Circular Matchmaking

Takes the brand new box from the apartment and gives it directly to the nearest shopkeeper who needs it.
