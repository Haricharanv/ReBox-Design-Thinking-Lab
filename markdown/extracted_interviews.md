ReBox – Empathy Survey Questionnaire
Questionnaire 1 – Urban Resident / Online Shopper
Name: Aditi ShetkarAge: 21Locality: Bidar

4. Do you feel guilty throwing away a good-condition cardboard box? Why?

Ans: I might consider donating used boxes if I had enough time to visit a nearby place. Convenience would matter a lot.
8. If a drop-off bin was placed in your apartment basement or near the BBMP dry waste collection point, would you use it?

9. Would you scan a QR code to log your drop-off, or is that too much effort?
Ans: I might scan a QR code sometimes, but only if it’s quick and not too much effort. Otherwise, I may skip it.
10. What would motivate you to participate?
Ans: I’d be motivated mainly by the environmental impact. Knowing I’m helping the environment would encourage me.
11. How comfortable are you using a mobile app for something like this?
Ans: I’m very comfortable using a mobile app for something like this. In fact, I think it would be really helpful.
12. Would you recommend such a service to your flatmates or neighbors? Why or why not?
Ans: Yes, I would recommend this service to people around me. It seems useful and beneficial for everyone.
