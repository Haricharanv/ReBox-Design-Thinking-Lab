5 WHYS
1. Why do boxes end up in landfills?
Residents throw them in dry waste bins.
2. Why do they throw them away?
Lack of space AND no easy alternative.
3. Why no easy alternative?
Irregular scrap visits & no collection system.
4. Why scrap guy doesn't redirect?
Sells by WEIGHT to pulp factories (crushing).
5. Why no direct collection?
NO organized system connects them directly.
ROOT CAUSE:
Ecosystem is optimized for RECYCLING, not REUSE. Zero infrastructure connects generators (residents) with needers (vendors).
