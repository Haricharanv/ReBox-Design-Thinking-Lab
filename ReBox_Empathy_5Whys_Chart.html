Vendor Empathy Map

👨‍🌾
Says
  • "Cardboard prices keep going up."
  • "My margins are disappearing."
Thinks
  • "I wish I could get cheap, strong boxes locally."
  • "How can I cut operational costs?"
Does
  • Travels 20km to buy new boxes in bulk.
  • Reuses boxes until they completely break.
Feels
  • Stressed about rising wholesale rates.
  • Helpless regarding paper material prices.
5 WHYS
1. Why do good boxes end up in landfills?
Because residents throw them in dry waste bins.
2. Why do they throw them away?
Because they lack space AND have no easy alternative.
3. Why is there no easy alternative?
Because kabadiwala visits are irregular & no collection system exists.
4. Why doesn't the scrap guy redirect to vendors?
Because he sells by WEIGHT to pulp factories (crushing is easier).
5. Why don't vendors collect from apartments?
Because NO organized system connects them directly.
ROOT CAUSE:
The ecosystem is optimized for RECYCLING, not REUSE. There is zero infrastructure connecting box-generators (residents) with box-needers (vendors).
